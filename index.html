Textile Architecture

Human movement first. Fiber second.

Fast fashion relies on petroleum polyester and microplastic elastane to force stretch — creating synthetic traps that pill and suffocate skin. We engineer architectural drape entirely from natural fibers.

The Normandy Flax Provenance

Our long-staple flax is harvested along the Atlantic coast of Normandy. Naturally thermoregulating and carbon-negative, it requires zero irrigation and zero petroleum fertilizers.

01

Long-Staple Flax & Raw Wild Silk

Hollow-core Normandy flax fibers offer natural capillary cooling and moisture wicking, while raw tussah silk introduces fluid tensile strength and drape without synthetic stiffeners or chemical starches.

02

Low-Impact Botanical & Mineral Vat Dyes

We dye our yarns using madder root, walnut husks, chestnut tannins, and earthen iron clays. The result is rich, sunbaked chromatic depth without heavy-metal mordants or petroleum azo dyes.

03

Zero Synthetic Polymers Guarantee

Every garment is 100% biodegradable at end of life. Zero polyester, acrylic, or elastane blends. Instead, we achieve natural mechanical ease through precision diagonal 45° bias cuts and open slub weaving.
