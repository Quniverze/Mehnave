A Note from Khadeeja Mehna

“Mehnave was born from a deep love for the gentle rhythm of Malabar — where the sea breeze meets the softness of river-washed handloom cottons. Growing up in Calicut, I watched how traditional textiles carry our stories, our warmth, and our identity. With Mehnave, my intention is simple: to create ethnic silhouettes that breathe with you, honouring generations of teakwood block printing and pure botanical dyes, while feeling effortless, modern, and gracefully modest. Every ensemble is crafted in small, unhurried batches — made to be lived in, cherished, and passed down.”
Khadeeja Mehna
Founder & Creative Director · Calicut, Kerala
The Malabar Textile Heritage

Breathable cotton. Hand-pressed blocks. Natural dyes.

Fast fashion traps heat with synthetic polyester and stiff chemical starches. At Mehnave, our craft is rooted in ancient Malabar river-washing, organic plant pigments, and wooden block artistry.

The Calicut Handloom Legacy

Calicut has been the world’s textile crossroads for over eight centuries. We work directly with Kerala master artisans who hand-carve teak printing blocks and hand-dip cottons in pure herbal dyes.

01

Pure Breathable Malabar Cottons

Native long-staple cottons woven on traditional wooden pit looms across Kerala. The open-slub weave allows air to circulate freely, offering natural thermoregulation in tropical humidity.

02

Teakwood Hand-Block Printing

Each motif is carved into seasoned teak blocks and hand-aligned with measured rhythm. Subtle organic variations in the stamp honor the human touch behind every garment.

03

Chemical-Free Botanical Dyes

Colors coaxed from madder root, turmeric ochre, indigo leaves, and pomegranate rind. Free from harsh chemical fixatives or petroleum azo compounds — pure and gentle on sensitive skin.
