Formulation Architecture

Skin biology first. Pigment second.

Most cosmetics rely on cheap cyclic silicones to mimic slip — creating an occlusive film that traps heat and pills by noon. We built a proprietary biocompatible lipid matrix.

The Oregon Lipid Sourcing

Our cold-pressed Limnanthes Alba (meadowfoam) is harvested in the Willamette Valley, retaining 98% long-chain fatty acids that naturally mirror the skin's sebum profile.

01

Biocompatible Meadowfoam & Squalane Matrix

Conventional foundation silicones evaporate, stranding dry pigments inside pores. Our high-slip meadowfoam and olive-derived squalane fuse with your natural sebum, allowing pigments to flex fluidly with facial movement for 14 hours.

02

Sub-Micronized Earth Mineral Suspensions

We mill non-nano iron oxides and natural micas down to sub-micron particles, then individually wet-coat each platelet in plant lipid esters. The result is pure, non-chalky chromatic depth without talc or titanium flash-back.

03

Jeju Fermented Camellia Active

Through a 21-day anaerobic microbial fermentation, raw Camellia Japonica seed yields low-molecular-weight polyphenols. This shields delicate epidermal lipids from urban particulate matter and oxidative discoloration.
